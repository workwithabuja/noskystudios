Anatomy

Music analysis for iPhone: key, tempo, chords, lyrics and song parts.

By NoSky Studios Ltd · iOS app

About the app

Anatomy listens to music — live through the microphone, in your headphones, or from a file you import — names it, and helps you understand and play with it: key, tempo, loudness, structure, chords, and lyrics. It splits a song into its parts, changes its pitch and speed, and blends one song into the next in key.

Analysis runs entirely on your device. No account, no sign-in, no ads. Anatomy Pro is one purchase, yours for good — no subscription.
