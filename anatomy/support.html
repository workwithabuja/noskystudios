Contact us

For help with Anatomy, questions about the App Store, or feedback, email appstore@noskystudios.ng. Tell us your iPhone model, your iOS version, the Anatomy version (in Settings → About inside the app), and what happened. A screenshot or screen recording helps too.

App Store purchase and refund requests are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com. For privacy questions, email privacy@noskystudios.ng.

Common questions

How do I restore Anatomy Pro?

Open Settings in Anatomy and tap Restore Purchase, or tap Restore on the Anatomy Pro screen. Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple Account you bought it with. Anatomy Pro is a one-time purchase, so restoring it never charges you again.

What does Anatomy Pro include?

Anatomy Pro unlocks the Studio: separating a song into its parts, key and tempo with chords, EQ, Remix, the song's Overview, and saving to Files.

The player, your library, listening to name a song, lyrics, and Auto Mix are free. Anatomy Pro is a one-time purchase, with no trial and no subscription.

Why can't Anatomy hear music playing in my headphones?

iOS doesn't let apps hear what other apps play into your headphones. To use Anatomy with that song, play it out loud near your iPhone so Anatomy can listen through the microphone, or import the song's file into Anatomy.

Where are my songs stored?

Only on your iPhone. Anatomy has no account and no cloud, and your recordings and imported songs are never uploaded. They're included in your iPhone's own backups. Deleting a song, or the app, removes it from your iPhone. See the Privacy Policy for the details.
