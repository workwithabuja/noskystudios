AppWatch

App Store rank, rating, review and sales tracker for iPhone and iPad.

By NoSky Studios Ltd · iOS app

About the app

AppWatch follows how apps perform on the App Store — chart positions in every country, ratings, reviews, prices, and versions — and alerts you when something moves. Link your own App Store Connect account to see your sales, proceeds, and analytics alongside.

AppWatch has no server, no accounts, and no analytics. Everything it fetches goes straight between your device and Apple, and your App Store Connect key stays in your iCloud Keychain.

Support

For help with AppWatch, questions about the App Store, or feedback, email appstore@noskystudios.ng. Include your device, iOS version, and the AppWatch version (in the app's Settings) and we can usually help on the first reply.

App Store purchase and refund requests are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com. For privacy questions, email privacy@noskystudios.ng.
