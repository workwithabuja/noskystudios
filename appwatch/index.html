Do I need an App Store Connect account?

No. Rankings, ratings, reviews and prices come from Apple's public App Store data, so you can watch any app, yours or anyone else's. An App Store Connect API key is only needed for your own sales, downloads and review replies.

How do I link App Store Connect?

In App Store Connect, go to Users and Access, then Integrations, and create a Team API key. In AppWatch, open Settings, tap Link App Store Connect, and add the Issuer ID, Key ID, the .p8 file and your Vendor Number. Replying to reviews needs a key with Admin or Customer Support access.

Can I try it without a key?

Yes. Tap Try Demo Data to load Example Inc., a made-up account with apps, charts, reviews and sales. Everything in it is generated on your device, and you can remove it in Settings at any time.

What does AppWatch Unlimited cost?

US$9.99 once, or the equivalent price in your country's App Store. There's no subscription. The free app has every feature with one watched app; Unlimited lets you watch as many as you like.

I bought Unlimited on another device. How do I get it back?

Open the AppWatch Unlimited screen in Settings and tap Restore Purchase, signed in with the same Apple Account you bought it with.

How do I get a refund?

App Store purchases and refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com. We can't issue refunds directly, but we're happy to help with anything else at appstore@noskystudios.ng.

Where is my data, and how do I delete it?

On your device. Removing an app deletes its history, and Settings has Erase All Data, plus Erase and Unlink Keys to remove your API keys from iCloud Keychain as well. We hold no copy of your data.

Why don't some charts update every time?

Apple limits how often its charts can be read, so AppWatch spreads its checks out and runs a Full Check of every country on a schedule. It catches up on its own when Apple allows.
