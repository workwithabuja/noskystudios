Private by design

No bank login. Ever.

Kelora never connects to your bank and has no account to sign into. The subscriptions, prices, dates and notes you enter stay on your device, and back up privately to your own iCloud — which we cannot read.

  • No sign-in, no account, no password
  • No analytics and no tracking of any kind
  • Nothing sold, nothing shared with anyone
  • Works offline; optional iCloud sync across your devices

The only optional network requests are for currency rates and brand logos. They send a currency code or a brand name — never your financial data. Read the full Kelora Privacy Policy.

Does Kelora connect to my bank?

No, and it never will. You add subscriptions yourself — which takes a few minutes once — and Kelora does the tracking, the maths and the reminding from there. Nothing about your bank ever touches the app.

Where is my data stored?

On your device. If you turn on iCloud sync, a copy also goes to your own private iCloud storage, which only you can read. We have no servers holding your financial data and no way to see it.

Can I use it in my own currency?

Yes. Kelora handles multiple currencies at once and converts them for your totals, so a subscription billed in dollars still shows up correctly alongside one billed in naira, euros or pounds.

What languages does it speak?

47, including Arabic, Hebrew and Urdu with proper right-to-left layout. Kelora follows whatever language your iPhone is set to.

How do I get a refund?

App Store purchase and refund requests are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com. For Kelora support, contact appstore@noskystudios.ng.

I found a bug, or I want a feature.

Please send it to appstore@noskystudios.ng. Kelora is actively developed and a good deal of what's in it started as someone's email.
