Reporting a problem

Include these and we can usually reproduce it on the first try.

  • Your device (for example, iPhone 15 Pro or iPad Air)
  • Your iOS or iPadOS version — Settings › General › About
  • The Kelora version — Settings › About, inside the app
  • What you were doing when it went wrong
  • A screenshot or screen recording, if you have one

Please don't send screenshots containing anything you'd rather we didn't see. We only need enough to find the bug.

FAQ

Common questions

Getting started

How do I add my subscriptions?

Tap the + button on the Home screen, then type the service name — Kelora suggests the logo and category for you. Set the price, the billing cycle and the next renewal date, and you're done. Most people get their whole list in within about ten minutes.

Does Kelora connect to my bank?

No, and it never will. There is no bank login, no Open Banking connection and no card scanning. You enter the subscriptions yourself; Kelora does the tracking, the totals and the reminders from there.

Can I import from a receipt or an email?

Yes. Share a subscription confirmation email or receipt to Kelora from the iOS share sheet, and it will read the service, the price and the billing cycle out of it and offer to add it. You always confirm before anything is saved.

How do I add something that isn't billed monthly?

Kelora handles weekly, monthly, quarterly, yearly and custom cycles. Annual plans are spread across the months in your totals, so a yearly commitment appears as a steady monthly amount rather than one alarming spike in the month it renews.

Reminders & notifications

I'm not getting renewal reminders.

Check that notifications are allowed: Settings › Notifications › Kelora on your device, with Allow Notifications turned on. Then open Kelora's own Settings and confirm reminders are enabled and set to the number of days' notice you want.

Also worth checking: Focus modes and Scheduled Summary can hold notifications back until later in the day, which can make a reminder look like it never arrived.

Can I be reminded earlier, or less often?

Yes. You choose how many days before a renewal you're warned, and you can turn reminders off entirely for individual subscriptions you don't want to hear about.

What is the Live Activity?

On the day something renews, Kelora can show a Live Activity on your Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island with the service name and the amount. It disappears on its own once the day is over.

Data, privacy & backup

Where is my data stored?

On your device. If you turn on iCloud sync, a copy also goes to your own private iCloud storage, readable only by you. We hold no copy of your financial data on any server and have no way to see it.

How do I move to a new iPhone?

If iCloud sync is on, sign in to the same Apple Account on the new device, install Kelora, and your data appears. If it's off, make a backup from Kelora's Settings first, then restore it on the new device.

Do you track me or run analytics?

No. There is no analytics SDK, no advertising identifier and no third-party tracking in Kelora. The full details are in the Kelora Privacy Policy.

How do I delete everything?

Deleting the app removes the data on that device. To clear the iCloud copy as well, turn off iCloud sync and delete Kelora's data from Settings › Apple Account › iCloud › Manage Storage. Since we never hold your data, there is nothing for us to delete on our side.

App Store

What does Kelora Pro include?

Kelora Pro is intended to unlock the full feature set, including unlimited subscriptions and bills, Home Screen widgets, Live Activities, iCloud sync across your devices, budgets, savings tracking and deeper insights.

How do I restore access on another device?

Sign in with the same Apple Account, open Kelora's Settings and tap Restore Purchases. If it still doesn't unlock, email appstore@noskystudios.ng.

How do I get a refund?

App Store purchase and refund requests are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com. For Kelora support, contact appstore@noskystudios.ng.

How do I cancel a subscription I'm tracking?

Kelora shows you what to cancel; the cancelling itself happens wherever you signed up. For subscriptions billed through Apple, go to your Apple subscriptions. For everything else, use that service's own website or app.

Languages & accessibility

What languages does Kelora support?

47, including Arabic, Hebrew and Urdu with full right-to-left layout. Kelora follows the language your device is set to; you don't have to choose it in the app.

Does it work with VoiceOver and larger text?

Yes. Kelora supports Dynamic Type all the way up to the largest accessibility sizes without cutting text off, and every control is labelled for VoiceOver. If you hit something that reads badly or clips, please report it — we treat that as a bug.
